How to play on a winter day

Winter bingo is built for the hour when everyone is damp, tired and warming up. It works well after sledding, during indoor recess or when the power flickers and screens are off.

  1. Hand out cards and a mug of small counters such as marshmallows or paper snowflakes.
  2. Read a square aloud or look out the window for matches.
  3. Players cover the squares they have seen or done this winter.
  4. The first row gets the first mug of cocoa.
  5. Switch cards and play another round with a different caller.

Three cold weather ideas

  • Window watch. Place the card by a window and cover squares as things pass by, such as a cardinal or a plow. It turns a snow day into a patient game.
  • Indoor recess edition. Teachers use a short list of classroom-friendly squares and let students cover them as they build, read or draw.
  • Warm climate winter. In places without snow, play with squares such as a sweater morning, a bonfire and a hot drink, and add your own local signs of the season.

Tips for cold days

Pair the game with a snack, since children play longer with a warm drink. Keep drinks away from the cards, or use sheet protectors.

If you go outside, keep it short. A quick walk to spot tracks and icicles is better than a long one in the wind.

Teachers can use the squares as writing prompts. Ask each child to choose a square and write two lines about it.

Printing for winter

Light blue or silver paper suits the theme, and the ink saver option keeps the grid pale. Two cards per page gives roomy squares for gloved hands that have just come in.

A dry-erase pocket makes a handy reusable set for a family that plays all season.

FAQ

Can I use winter bingo in a warm climate?

Yes. Edit the list to match what winter looks like in your area, such as cooler mornings or a rainy season.

Is this a holiday game?

No. The squares avoid specific holidays so any family or class can use them. For Christmas and Hanukkah, see the dedicated pages.

How can I make it shorter?

Choose a 3x3 grid and a smaller list, and rounds finish in under five minutes.
