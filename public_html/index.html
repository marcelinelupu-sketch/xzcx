Why frogsdream

  • It's free to use. Every tool on this site is free, and so is printing the results for home, classroom and party use.
  • No sign-up. There is no account to create and no email to hand over. Open a page and start.
  • Works on phones and Chromebooks. The tools run in any modern browser, so you can set up a game on your phone and print it from the school computer.
  • Letter and A4. Switch the paper size with one tap, and the sheets resize to fit.
  • Friendly to your printer. Ink saver mode prints clean black-and-white sheets that copy well.
  • Share the exact set. A share link brings back the same words and the same cards, which helps when a co-teacher or co-host needs a copy.

frogsdream is a small independent project. If something doesn't print right or a word list has a mistake, let us know and we will fix it.
