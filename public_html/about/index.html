About Frog's Dream

Frog's Dream is a small, independent website of free printable games and charts. You type or pick a list, press a button, and get clean sheets ready for the printer: bingo cards, word searches, scavenger hunts, word scrambles, bedtime routine charts and reward charts.

Who it's for

The site is written for the grown-ups who run the fun:

  • Teachers and ESL teachers who need a vocabulary game for tomorrow morning, in the right paper size for their school.
  • Parents setting up a rainy-day hunt, a road trip game or a bedtime chart for a little one.
  • Party and shower hosts who want guests mixing and laughing without buying a boxed game.
  • Community groups, libraries and care homes running a bingo afternoon with a projector and the online caller.

How it's made

Frog's Dream is run by one person in Poland. It is not part of a larger company, and nobody pays us to feature anything.

The tools, word lists and articles were written with AI assistance and reviewed for accuracy. Where a page states a fact, such as recommended sleep hours for young children, it links to the source so you can check it yourself. If you spot a misspelled word, an unsuitable item in a list or anything that looks wrong, please tell us and we will correct it.

There are no reviews, ratings or testimonials on this site, because we would rather you try a tool for a minute and judge it yourself.

What you can do with the printables

Everything you make here is free to print and use at home, in your classroom and at parties, showers, clubs and community events. You don't need to credit us beyond the small line already printed at the bottom of each sheet. The one thing we ask is that you don't sell the printed sheets or files as your own product. The terms have the details.

The generators run in your browser, so the words you type stay on your device unless you choose to share a link. There is no account to create.

How the site pays for itself

Hosting and the domain cost money, so the site may show ads from Google. We also sell an optional Mega Pack of ready-made PDFs. None of this changes what is free here. The disclosure page explains how it works.

Why a frog?

Because a sleepy little frog who loves games felt like good company for bedtime charts and bingo nights alike. You will find the frog waving from the corner of most printables. If you'd rather print without it, switch off the frog stamp in any tool.

Get in touch

Ideas for new themes, a word list for a holiday we've missed, or a printer that refuses to cooperate: the contact page has our email address, and we usually reply within a few days.
