What you will find here

The bingo guides begin with the basics: how to play bingo, what a 75-ball and a 90-ball game do differently, and a gallery of winning patterns to make a short game longer or a long game shorter.

For teachers, there are ideas for classroom bingo and a guide to ESL vocabulary games that you can use with a beginner group tomorrow morning.

For families, we have a guide to planning a scavenger hunt, a calm look at the toddler bedtime routine with links to published sleep guidance, and a practical comparison of Letter and A4 printing.

How we write them

The content on this site was written with AI assistance and reviewed for accuracy. Where a guide states a fact about sleep, safety or health, it links to the source so you can check it yourself. Bedtime and child health material is general information, not medical advice.

Each guide connects back to a tool, so you can read about a pattern and then go straight to the bingo card generator or the bedtime routine chart to try it. If something is unclear or out of date, tell us through the contact page.

We add guides when readers ask for them, so if there is a game you would like explained, send a note.

FAQ

Are the guides free to read?

Yes. Every guide is open to read without signing up.

Is the bedtime guide medical advice?

No. It shares general recommendations and links to the original sources. For concerns about your child's sleep, speak to your pediatrician.
