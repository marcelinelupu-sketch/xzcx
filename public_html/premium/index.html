The Frog's Dream Mega Pack

Everything on this site is free to make and print, and it will stay that way. The Mega Pack is for the times when you would rather not generate anything at all: one download with every theme already laid out as print-ready PDFs, in both US Letter and A4, with answer keys and caller sheets included.

One payment of $7. No subscription.

What's inside

  • Themed bingo: all 51 themed bingo pages from the site, each with 40 unique cards (5 x 5, or 4 x 4 for the kindergarten themes), printed 2 per page (1 per page for Find Someone Who, so there is room for names), plus a caller checklist for each theme.
  • 75-ball bingo: 100 unique cards.
  • 90-ball UK bingo: 20 strips of 6 tickets, where each strip uses every number from 1 to 90 exactly once.
  • Word searches: all 36 word search themes at 2 difficulty levels each, every puzzle with its answer key.
  • Word scrambles: all 10 word scramble themes, each with an answer key.
  • Scavenger hunts: all 18 scavenger hunt lists.
  • Charts: 6 bedtime routine and reward chart designs, each in 4 colors.
  • A one-page Start here sheet that explains the folders and printing settings.

Every file comes in both US Letter and A4, as one PDF per theme and paper size, sorted into folders inside a single ZIP download. The PDFs are vector files, so text and lines stay crisp at any print size.

Who it's for

The pack suits teachers who want a term's worth of games in one folder, party hosts who would like to print and go, and anyone with a slow connection or a school computer that blocks new websites. If you only need one set of cards for one event, the free generators already do that well, and you don't need the pack.

FAQ

Do I need the Mega Pack to use the generators?

No. Every generator on this site is free and makes up to 30 bingo cards, or up to 10 puzzles, each time you press Generate. You can generate as many times as you like. The pack only saves you the generating and gives you more cards per theme in one place.

What format are the files?

PDF files inside one ZIP download. Open the ZIP on a computer, then open any PDF and print it like a normal document. Each theme has a Letter file and an A4 file, so pick the one that matches the paper in your printer.

Can I change the words in the pack?

The PDFs are finished pages and can't be edited. If you want your own words, use the free generator for that theme, which starts with the same list and lets you edit every line.

How do I get a refund?

If a file doesn't open, prints wrongly or is missing something, email us within 14 days of buying and tell us what went wrong. We will fix the file or refund you through Lemon Squeezy, whichever you prefer.
